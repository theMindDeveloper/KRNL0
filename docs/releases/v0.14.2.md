# krnl0 v0.14.2

All your boards, always in reach.

## Fixed

- The board menu (next to the KRNL0 logo) now shows in Easy mode too, on your computer and on your phone. Before, Easy hid it while you had one board, so a board that came from your server, like one you started on the website, could not be opened.
- The menu keeps up by itself: a board made, renamed or deleted on another device shows up without reloading.

## Install

KRNL0 updates itself. New here? Download **KRNL0-Setup-0.14.2.exe** below.
