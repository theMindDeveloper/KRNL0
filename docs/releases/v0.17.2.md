# krnl0 v0.17.2

Touch that works: hold, then drag.

## New

**Hold, then drag.** On an iPad, a phone or a touch screen, hold a finger still on a plan, an event or a habit for a moment and it lifts off: drag it to a new time and let go. A quick swipe still scrolls. The same hold draws a new box on empty time, and carries a to-do or a habit onto the Calendar.

**Stretch with a finger.** A plan's top and bottom edges are wide enough for a fingertip. On a short plan, hold its lower half and pull to make it longer.

**Carry it to the Calendar.** On the phone, hold a to-do, carry it over the Calendar tab and wait a moment: the Calendar opens, and you drop it on the time you want.

## Fixed

- Dragging a plan with a finger no longer moves the view instead of the plan, in My layout and on the canvas.
- Pinching the Calendar zooms only its hours, not the canvas behind it or the whole page.
- Holding a task to carry it no longer opens its menu as well. Hold and lift in place to open the menu.
- The Clock: a drag round the dial with a finger now stretches as you go.
- The on-screen keyboard no longer covers what you type into: the card moves up above it.
- A new board, or your board opened on a new iPad for the first time, starts on My layout as Reset arranges it.

## Install

KRNL0 on Windows updates itself. New here? Download **KRNL0-Setup-0.17.2.exe** (Windows) or **KRNL0-0.17.2-arm64.dmg** (Mac) below. Your KRNL0 server shows the new website once you update it: click **saved** in the top bar, then **Update server**.

**Mac, first start:** KRNL0 isn't signed by Apple yet. Open the .dmg and drag KRNL0 into Applications, then open it. macOS says it can't check the app: click **Done**, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. After that it opens like any other app. Mac copies show "KRNL0 X.Y.Z is out" when there's a new version.
