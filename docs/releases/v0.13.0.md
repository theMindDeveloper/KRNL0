# krnl0 v0.13.0

Your board on every device. Connect KRNL0 to a **server you run yourself**, and every computer you connect keeps the same boards — and your phone opens them in its browser.

## New

**Your board, on every device.**
- Run your own KRNL0 server at home (Docker, or one command on Linux). Connect it from the **saved** badge in the top bar: its address, the fingerprint the server shows, your password, once.
- Boards, the list of boards, plans, habits, the calendar, the timer's time, pictures and files sync within a second. Your view, layout and open board stay on each computer.
- **Offline is fine.** Changes wait on the computer and go when the server is back. Edits made on two devices at the same moment are both kept.
- The saved badge tells you: **saved · synced**, **syncing**, **saved · offline**. A click shows your devices and **Sync now**.
- Safe by design: every connection is encrypted, the app checks the server's fingerprint, and its key stays in your system's keychain.
- A tutorial shows it: Help › Tutorials › **Saved and synced**.

**Open it on your phone.**
- Your server serves KRNL0 to any browser after you log in.
- **On a phone:** Today (the timer, your plans, your habits), Calendar (with the Clock), Habits, Insights and the Canvas, and **Focus** with the timer full size while it runs.
- **On an iPad:** the same, with a side rail and Today in two columns. In a computer's browser: the Station and Canvas you know.
- Hold a finger on a plan or a habit for the same menu as a right-click. One finger moves the canvas, two zoom.
- The terminal and the AI stay in the desktop app.

## Also new
