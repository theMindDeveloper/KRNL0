# krnl0 v0.15.0

A Calendar you can read, events that happen once, one way to move everything onto it, your day on your phone, and your layout your way.

## New

**A Calendar you can read.**
- Events and plans are calm cards with their whole name, time and room. The day fits the window, today has its own circle, and the red now line is strong on today only.
- Zoom with a pinch, **Ctrl + wheel**, or by dragging the hour numbers.
- Click a plan or an event for its card; right-click it (or **Details**) to change it. One card or menu at a time, everywhere.
- Each block shows what it is in its corner: a plan, an event, a repeating event or a habit. A missed plan shows its replan in red.
- The menu button sits in the Calendar's header in every view, and the thin line at the left of each day shows what you recorded.

**Events that happen once.**
- An appointment, an exam, a train: type it into the Calendar's field, like **Dentist fri 15:00**, and it is added. Without a time its card opens on **Once**; "every …" opens it on **Repeats**.
- The Calendar adds events; plans come from your Todo. A one-time event moves its date when you carry it to another day, and can be made to repeat (and back).
- Command: `krnl event add "Dentist" --on 2026-11-27 --at 15:00`

**Pick it up, put it anywhere.**
- Todo rows, plan cards, habit and event cards on the board, Habit rows and Month chips all move onto the Calendar the same way, with a line showing the exact time. The Clock follows along.
- Pull a block's edge to change its length: it shows its new times while you pull, and nothing changes when you let go.
- Drags are smoother and a drop is on screen the moment you let go.

**Your day, on your phone.**
- Today shows your day in order: plans, classes and timed habits, with the one happening now or next opened up and Focus on it. **Start timer** is at the top.
- The Calendar, Habits, Insights and the board are full screen, one tap away. A long press opens a card's menu.

**Your layout, your way.**
- The layout menu in the top bar: **My layout**, arranged your way and kept as you leave it (**Reset** gives Timer, Clock and Todos over a full-width Calendar), and the **Free canvas**.
- On the Free canvas you can make the Calendar bigger. Each computer keeps its own window places and sizes; your plans sync as before.
- Commands: `krnl settings layout classic|free|reset`, `krnl window size calendar 900`

## Also new

- Lines between cards now only show an order. They no longer move, push or unschedule plans; **Add next** plans the new one right after. Every time on your board stays as it was.
- The Clock's dial fills its window, with today's plans in a small list below.
- Analytics in plain words: one band of numbers with changes in hours and minutes, Focus per day, and lists and bars instead of radar and pie charts. It follows light and dark.
- An event card's **Show** goes straight to the event; an event that has ended shows the last time it happened.
- The ambient radio is drawn in the app's own window style, in light and dark.
- The Calendar and Events tutorials are rewritten, and a new one shows the layouts.
- Fixed: events longer than 8 hours keep their length; "22:00-02:00" is read as a time; the Calendar opens at the right hour when zoomed in.
