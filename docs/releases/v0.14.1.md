# krnl0 v0.14.1

Your devices now keep the change you made last.

## Sync

- Change the same thing on two devices while they are apart (the time of a plan on your laptop on the train, then again later on your phone), and the change you made **last** is the one that stays, whichever device gets back online first. Before, the first device back won.
- Delete a plan, a habit or a window on one device while another changes it, and it stays, with the change. Nothing you worked on disappears.
- A device whose clock is off can't win by accident: KRNL0 corrects for it.
- Nothing to set up and nothing new on the screen. It works everywhere once your computers have 0.14.1 and your server has its next update (one click: **saved › Update server**).

## Fixed

- A phone that follows your board without the live connection took its own clock as the right time; it now reads your server's.

## Install

KRNL0 updates itself. New here? Download **KRNL0-Setup-0.14.1.exe** below.
