# krnl0 v0.17.1

Your phone on its side, a dock beside you.

## New

**Your phone on its side.** On your KRNL0 server's website, turn the phone sideways and it becomes a small dock: Todo on the left, the Clock on the right, and the timer in the corner. **Start** it there, or see what runs and **Stop** it.

**Choose what sits where.** Tap a window's name to put another one in its place: Todo, Timer, Clock, Calendar, Habits or Insights. On the iPad, the four windows of Today change the same way. Each phone and iPad keeps its own choice.

**More is a small menu.** On the phone, More opens a short list right at the tab: Insights and Canvas, one tap away.

## Fixed

- **Drag to add works with a finger.** On a phone or a touch screen, hold a finger still on empty time on the Calendar or the Clock, then drag. A quick swipe still scrolls.
- Settings on a phone: the option cards never run past their edges ("Overnight", "Crisp").
- The Clock's date is never cut off in a narrow window.
- On a phone held sideways, the Calendar's hours stay readable and scroll.

## Install

KRNL0 on Windows updates itself. New here? Download **KRNL0-Setup-0.17.1.exe** (Windows) or **KRNL0-0.17.1-arm64.dmg** (Mac) below. Your KRNL0 server shows the new website once you update it: click **saved** in the top bar, then **Update server**.

**Mac, first start:** KRNL0 isn't signed by Apple yet. Open the .dmg and drag KRNL0 into Applications, then open it. macOS says it can't check the app: click **Done**, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. After that it opens like any other app. Mac copies show "KRNL0 X.Y.Z is out" when there's a new version.
