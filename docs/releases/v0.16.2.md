# krnl0 v0.16.2

KRNL0 is on the Mac.

## New

- **KRNL0 for Mac.** Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and newer) can download **KRNL0-0.16.2-arm64.dmg** below or from krnl0.com. The floating timer floats over your other apps and full-screen windows, and the menu bar shows the running timer.

## Fixed

- `krnl pomo` commands in KRNL0's terminal work again in the installed app. Before, they stopped with "Cannot find module".
- The terminal's welcome banner shows KRNL0's version again instead of v0.0.0.

## Install

KRNL0 on Windows updates itself. New here? Download **KRNL0-Setup-0.16.2.exe** (Windows) or **KRNL0-0.16.2-arm64.dmg** (Mac) below.

**Mac, first start:** KRNL0 isn't signed by Apple yet. Open the .dmg and drag KRNL0 into Applications, then open it. macOS says it can't check the app: click **Done**, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. After that it opens like any other app. Mac copies show "KRNL0 X.Y.Z is out" when there's a new version.
