# krnl0 v0.11.3

A careful check of the whole app, and more than fifty fixes. Your board is safer, plans and numbers show up where they should, and pop-ups behave.

## Your board is safer

- If the file that holds your board ever gets damaged, KRNL0 writes the board you have open back to it, instead of starting from an empty one.
- Deleting a repeating event from the terminal never deletes a habit with the same name.
- "Clear focus history" stays cleared after a restart.

## Plans

- **Won't do** is undone with one Ctrl+Z, and a plan you won't do leaves the Calendar, the Clock, Welcome back and Analytics everywhere at once. A timer running on it stops.
- Adding a plan with its time is one Ctrl+Z.
- "Gym fri 18:00" typed on a Friday evening goes to next Friday, not into the past. In Easy, "Pay rent by fri" puts it on Friday.
- Reminders: a plan made just before it starts still gets its reminder, and after your laptop sleeps you are not told about plans that already started.

## Calendar, habits and the timer

- Restarting a repeating event you had ended keeps the weeks it was ended empty.
- **End after today** in the Repeating events list now shows "ends after today", with a Keep button.
- A late event with no length set runs its full hour past midnight.
- Your best streak is never shown lower than your current one. The Clock doesn't show a habit on its rest days.
- Time you log can't overlap a paused session (it was counted twice).
- Welcome back doesn't call a block that is still running "missed".
- The hours chart spreads a session over the hours it covered. Days when the clocks change count 23 or 25 hours.

## Pop-ups and keys

- Esc in the time or reminder list closes only the list, not the plan you were typing.
- After picking a time, your typing stays in the field.
- Ctrl+Z and Delete wait while a plan card is open.
- The mouse wheel scrolls inside windows instead of zooming the board.
- The tour's Next button works while a card is open.

## For the AI terminal

- `krnl help` and the assistant's instructions match the app, and many commands now do exactly what they say.
- New commands: `window show / hide / swap`, `pomo label`, `analytics set`, `media title`, `image alt`.

## Updating

KRNL0 0.11.1 and newer updates itself: you'll see *"KRNL0 0.11.3 is ready — Restart"*. If yours says *"Couldn't check for updates"* (the original 0.10.1 and 0.11.0) or is older, download **KRNL0-Setup-0.11.3.exe** below and run it once. It keeps your board. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app because the installer isn't code-signed yet: choose **More info → Run anyway**.
