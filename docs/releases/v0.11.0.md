# krnl0 v0.11.0

Start simple, and get shown around: KRNL0 now opens in **Easy mode** with a guide that walks you through your first day out loud. Plans, habits and repeating events live on one board, and the timer floats above everything you work in.

## New

**Easy mode, and a guide.** KRNL0 starts in Easy mode: plan your day, record what you do, see how it went. Advanced (tags, focus sessions, deadlines, chains, the terminal) is one switch away in Settings › Mode.
- The first time you open KRNL0, a guide walks you through your first day — out loud.
- Every window has its own short tour, written separately for Easy and Advanced, and spoken. Find them in the orb's menu. The speaker button mutes the voice.
- What you make in a tour is practice: it is not counted, and at the end you choose to remove it or keep it.

**Plans, habits and events on one board.**
- A plan has one **When** — a day and a time, or just a day — and an optional **deadline**. Plans with a day but no time sit in the **ANY TIME** strip on the Calendar; deadlines show as small flags.
- **Repeating events** (a class, a work shift) sit on the Calendar every week and are never ticked. Skip one, move one, end it after a day. They can run past midnight.
- **Habits and events get cards** on the board next to your plans. On a habit, today's square ticks it (or adds one: "8 glasses a day"). An event shows the next date it happens, with **Skip this one** and **Show**.
- **Show** lights up a plan on the board and on the Calendar at once.
- Double-click empty hours on the Calendar to add a plan; the **Repeating** button adds a class or shift.

**A timer that floats.** Minimise KRNL0 while the timer runs and a small timer stays above your other windows — in three looks (Dial, Capsule, Box). The time left also shows on the taskbar (Windows) or next to the clock (Mac). Pause, take a break or stop from either.

**Ask the orb.** Ask anything: the orb answers with Claude Code (when it is installed) and can change your board for you. If Claude Code isn't installed or signed in, it says so and helps you sign in.

## Also new

- **Habits:** aim for 3 times a week on any days, a daily goal ("20 pages"), rest days that keep a streak, Week / Month / Year views, archive and restore.
- **Plans:** a reminder before a plan starts (even with the window closed), and **Won't do** for things you drop — with Undo.
- **Quick add:** "Gym tomorrow 18:00 1h", "Pay rent fri", "Meeting 14:00-15:30", "in 2 hours", "at noon".
- **Close the day:** in the evening, one card with what you did today.
- **Welcome back:** after two or more days away, a short list of what slipped, with one-click fixes.
- **Milestones** for streaks and focus sessions, with a Celebrations switch.
- **Align to grid** (Settings › Canvas): what you move settles into a neat spot, never on top of something — cards only, or everything.
- **Station:** a divider you let go near the line above or below clicks onto it.
- **Sounds:** three sound packs for ticks and the timer, with volume and an off switch.
- **Send feedback** from the app (Help › Send feedback).
- **Board backups:** a copy of your board is saved before every update (Settings › Updates).
- Easier to read everywhere: larger labels, stronger contrast in light and dark, line icons, and button text that never spills out.

## Fixed

- A new wire could snap onto a hidden (finished) card, and wires between hidden cards were still drawn.
- A tour's highlight could jump to another task when the board moved.
- Many smaller fixes across the Calendar, Clock, Pomo, Habits and Analytics — see the changelog.

## Updating

Windows installs update themselves; your board is kept and backed up first. Every older board opens in 0.11.
