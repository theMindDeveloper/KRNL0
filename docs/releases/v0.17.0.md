# krnl0 v0.17.0

Drag to add your time, name any time you spent, and pick your own colour.

## New

**Drag to add your time.**
- Drag across empty hours on the Calendar, or round the Clock.
- Time that has passed asks what it was, and logs it.
- Time ahead becomes a **task** (it goes to your Todo), an **event**, or something that **repeats**. The card remembers what you picked last.
- It lands on the edge of the block next to it; hold **Alt** to place it freely. In the week, it can run past midnight into the next day.

**Name any time you spent.**
- A game, a call, a walk: past time takes any name, not only a task.
- Names you used come back as you type, even from their first letters ("aoe" finds "Age of Empires 2").
- The kind button files the time under Play, Study or a kind you make.
- In Analytics, merge two names into one and their time adds up.

**Your colour, a smoother canvas.**
- Settings › Appearance › **Accent colour**: pick one of eight, or type your own. KRNL0 keeps it readable.
- The canvas moves more smoothly: no lag when you start to pan, and sharp text while you zoom.

## Also new

- **One mode.** Easy mode is gone: everyone gets the whole of KRNL0. If you used Easy, your timer stays a stopwatch. New boards start with the stopwatch too.
- The Clock: a drag carries on round the dial into the other half of the day, or into tomorrow.
- Drags move in 5-minute steps and catch on the time now. A drag that starts in the past stops at now.
- The Calendar has calmer hour rows and less room on top; the Google note can be hidden.
- A new card never lands on a window or on top of another card.
- The Calendar, Clock and Timer tours show the new parts.
- Commands: `krnl pomo add --text "name" [--tag kind]`, `krnl time rename`, `krnl theme accent`.
