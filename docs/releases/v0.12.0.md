# krnl0 v0.12.0

One board per project, and one look for every window. Your canvases became **separate boards**, each with its own plans, habits and time. Every window now uses the same header, the same view switch and the same buttons, and the cards for a plan, a habit and an event are one design you can read from top to bottom.

## New

**One board per project.** Make a board for uni, one for work, one for home.
- Each board has its own todos, habits, calendar, timer history, analytics, notes and drawings. Nothing is shared between boards.
- The name in the top bar opens your boards: switch, rename, delete, or make a new one. A new board starts empty, or with a copy of your habits starting from zero.
- **Your existing canvases became boards by themselves.** The first one keeps every plan, habit and all your history; the others keep their own notes and drawings. A copy of your board from before is kept, and a card explains it once.
- **One timer at a time.** A timer left running stays in its board; a note in the top bar takes you back to it.
- **Reminders reach you from every board**, with the board's name.
- A voiced tutorial shows it: the orb's menu › Separate boards (Advanced).

**The same place, in every window.**
- Every window has the same header (its name, one fact, the views, the settings), the same row for moving in time, and the same footer, in normal letters.
- The **Timer** has one calm face with a clock font of its own, its state in the header, and its settings as a list.
- **Two styles, Paper and Pro**, chosen in Settings. The style picker left the board.
- Narrow windows keep their full name; the views get tighter instead of running over the edge.

**A day, a time, a deadline. One card.**
- Adding a plan, a habit, an event or logged time uses one kind of card: a short list of rows.
- Pick the day with one press (Today, Tomorrow, Monday, or a date), add a time, a length or a deadline. The deadline opens in the same card.
- Click elsewhere and your change is kept. Escape and the close button cancel.

## Also new

- **Two volumes** in Settings › Sounds: app sounds and the AI voice, each with its own slider. The voice is quieter by default.
- **The Calendar week** wraps long names instead of cutting them; the **Year view** fills its window.
- **Connectors only on task cards.** Notes, images and frames have none. Wires you drew between other cards stay in your board file but are no longer drawn.
- Switching boards shows no start-up screen and plays no greeting.
- **Commands:** `krnl board list | new | switch | rename | delete`, `krnl sound voice <0-100>`, `krnl tour start boards`.

## Fixed

- A plan card threw your change away if you clicked elsewhere instead of pressing Save.
- "One timer at a time" could be bypassed by starting from a task, the Calendar or the terminal.
- The first tutorial's "plan it" step pointed at a column that was not there in a narrow Calendar.
- Copied habits no longer bring old rest days, pauses or their start date along.
- The last underlined links are quiet buttons.

## Safer storage

- A board switch that fails half-way (a locked file, a full disk) is undone completely, and waits out a short lock first.
- Every board, not only the open one, gets a safety copy before an update.
- A lost list of boards is rebuilt from the boards on disk.

## Known limits

- Switching boards closes open terminal sessions.
- A timer left running in another board does not ring when it ends; you see it when you go back.
