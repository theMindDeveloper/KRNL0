## Short description (up to 100 characters)
Plan your day on one canvas: timer, todos, habits, calendar and notes in one place.

## Description
KRNL0 puts everything you use to plan your day on one calm, endless canvas.

- **A calendar you can read.** Drop a task on today and it lands where you want it. Drag it to start from the minute you are in.
- **Todos that become a plan.** Add a task, give it a time and a length, and see your whole day.
- **A focus timer.** Work in focused rounds, with breaks, next to your list.
- **Habits.** Keep the small things you repeat, and tick them off.
- **Your other calendars.** See Google, Apple (iCloud) and calendar-link events beside your plans.
- **Your plans on your phone.** Keep your day in step with your phone.
- **Yours.** Your board is saved on your computer first. Nothing leaves it unless you connect your own server.
