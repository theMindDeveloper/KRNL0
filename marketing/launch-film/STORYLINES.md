# Storylines — KRNL0 launch film (YouTube, ~2:45)

One thing: *your whole day, on one canvas* (the product's own line). One viewer, one Tuesday: plan it, do it, see how it went.

**A — "One Tuesday" (continuous take).** A single unbroken camera move across the real canvas from 7:00 to 21:00; each chapter a stop. Calm, documentary. First 2 s: ghost clock digits 07:00. Sound: soft pulse, strings. Risk: we already made this as a reel (onecanvas); long takes drag at 3 minutes.

**B — "Nine to One" (type carries the argument).** Giant kinetic type and colour fields cutting on the beat, the nine noisy tools collapsing into one. First 2 s: a word slot-machine. Risk: reads as a typographic ad, the product appears late (the 'nine' reel did this).

**C — "Dive into the core" (endless zoom) — PICKED.** The cube's lit core is the hook and the device: the old way fails (a stack of tools that don't know each other), the cards collapse into the cube on the beat, the core flares on the DROP and the camera dives through it into the real app. Every chapter is entered through an object that is already on screen (the typed plan becomes the calendar block; the timer's digit becomes the recorded bar; a habit square becomes the year grid; the canvas pulls back to the end-card cube). First 2 s: a macro of one dark voxel, a single lit pulse on the first beat. Sound: sparse sub + heartbeat pulse → silence dip → drop on the downbeat at 38.9 s → driving groove → breakdown (look back) → climax → one resonant hit on the logo. Why: the logo IS the idea (Kernel Zero), it gives the cinematic hook, and the product is proven in real UI.
