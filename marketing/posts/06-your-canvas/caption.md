# Instagram caption — 06 · Your canvas (7 slides)

Not another list. 🧠

Most planners give you a column of tasks and nothing else. KRNL0 gives you a canvas: your plan sits in a window, and everything around it is yours. A sticky note for the thing Prof. Meyer said. A shape. An image. A frame that groups a whole week's worth of ideas, so they move together when you drag it.

Seven tools, one key each: Select (V), Text (N), Sticky note (S), Shape (R), Image (I), Pen (P), Frame (F).

Want it tidy? My layout keeps your windows in a row. Want it loose? Free canvas lets you put anything anywhere. Ctrl + Shift + L (⌘ + Shift + L on Mac) flips between them, and each one stays as you left it.

Make room for how you think.
