Your to-do list says what. It never says how long. ⏱️

Planned 1h. Took 2h05. Sound familiar?

In KRNL0 you plan a task, press start on the timer, and the time lands on that task. Then Analytics sets planned against spent: per task, per day. In this demo week, tasks took ×1.47 their estimate. Now you can plan with real numbers.

Every evening a recap shows what got done, what was recorded and how it went against the plan. Every Monday, a look back at last week, day by day.
