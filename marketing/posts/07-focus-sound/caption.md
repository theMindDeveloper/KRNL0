Give your focus a soundtrack. 🎧

KRNL0 has a radio built right in. Tap Rain, Fire, Synth, Dark, White or Brown, stack a few together, and give each sound its own volume. Tune every sound with its own dials, or paste a YouTube link if you'd rather bring your own.

Start the Timer, press play, and the radio keeps playing above your canvas. Tuck it to the edge when you don't need it.
