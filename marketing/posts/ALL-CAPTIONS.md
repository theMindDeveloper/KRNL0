## 11-sound-of-focus  (8 slides)

Focus has a sound. 🎧

Some people work better with a steady sound in the background: a low hum, a soft hiss, rain on a window. KRNL0 has an ambient radio built in, and it makes six of them right inside the app:

🌙 Dark: deep, low drone
🟤 Brown: soft and heavy
⚪ White: an even hiss
🌧 Rain: gentle rainfall
🔥 Fire: a low roar with a crackle
🎹 Synth: slow chords that drift

Tap one to play it. Tap a second to layer it, and set each layer's volume on its own. Then press start on the timer and get to it.

Many people find a steady sound helps them tune out the room. Everyone's different, so try them and see what works for you.
