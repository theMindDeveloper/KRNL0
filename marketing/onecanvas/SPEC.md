# A — "One day, one take" (marketing cut)

GOAL:    someone who keeps tasks, notes and habits in different places sees that KRNL0 holds them side by
         side on one canvas, and that the things they do every day (drop a note next to a plan, drag a plan
         onto the calendar, tick a habit) are one gesture each. They leave knowing it is free, needs no account
         and keeps their plans on their computer, and tap "link in bio".
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, ~37 s. Narrated (ElevenLabs Eleven v4, tags), word captions, quiet
         effects, a minimal music bed (ElevenLabs Music, instrumental, 0.14 under the voice).

THE ONE THING: "Your whole day lives on one canvas — and it works by moving things around."
THE TAKE:      one unbroken recording of the real canvas. The camera is the app's own fit-view, filmed in slow
               motion; the ground changes with a top-to-bottom wipe, like the website's day sections.

BEATS (voice drives all timing; see scripts/plan.py)
  hook    "Your whole day, one canvas."         tight on Todos, then pulled out to Todos + Calendar
  notes   "Put anything next to your plan."     camera flies to the notes; a real sticky note is added from the dock
  drag    "Pick it up, put it anywhere."        a plan is lifted out of Todos and dropped on the calendar: it gets its time
  habits  "Habits you can see building."        three habit ticks; the streaks grow
  end     "One app instead of nine."            pull back to the whole row (the Timer running), then the end card

LOOK:    acid #c9f158 (hook, end), lavender #a98bf6 (notes), cyan #22d3ee (calendar), coral #ee8466 (habits).
         Geist 800 at 130 px / -5.5% headlines, Geist 600 46 px captions. The recording sits in the middle
         1200 px of the frame; its top and bottom edges dissolve into the composition's own ground.
         A cursor (black arrow, 36 css px) is drawn so every interaction reads.
BANNED:  cuts between shots (the camera never cuts); windows cropped by a card edge; the three unused windows;
         clutter on the canvas (five windows' worth only: Todos, Calendar, Habits, Timer, one frame of notes);
         claims the product does not make; effects louder than the voice.
QA:      frame 0 shows the headline and the real Todos; every beat's action is visible and lands on its word;
         ground seam invisible; phone sheet at 360 px; voice, effects and music measured.
