# KRNL0 reel — three storylines (pick one)

Common to all three: 1080×1920 Reels, 20–30 s, built in HyperFrames from the **website's own
components** (sections, cube, Today strip, time chips, K·R·N·L·0 tiles) plus the **real app** windows,
each shown whole and at 1.6–2× when it acts — never a cropped slice of a window. Sound: a music bed
(your track, or the free HeyGen catalog after sign-in) plus sparse effects from the bundled library:
key ticks on typing, a pop when a chip or block lands, a whoosh only on big moves, one riser into the
mark. Voiceover (ElevenLabs) can be laid on any of them later.

---

## C — "One sentence, everywhere" (recommended: one real plan a stranger can follow from start to finish)

- **Device / form:** one object across the app · object-to-object chain (every shot ends on the thing that opens the next).
- **What you see:** someone types one sentence — `Gym tomorrow 18:00 1h` — and it becomes real everywhere: a card in Todos, a block on the Calendar, an arc on the Clock, the task on the Timer, a tick on the Habit, a card on the Canvas. You feel the app doing the bookkeeping for you.
- **First 2 s:** frame 0 already shows the Todos input, big, with `Gym tomorrow` typed; `18:00 1h` types in with key ticks and the quick-add card snaps open (pop).
- **Beats:** typed sentence → it lands as a card → card drops onto the Calendar's Day view (the block grows to 18:00–19:00) → the block's colour sweeps round the Clock dial → Start session: Timer counts with it → streak ticks +1 → zoom out: the whole day on the canvas → "One app instead of nine." → K·R·N·L·0 tiles + download CTA.
- **Look:** the website's day colours as grounds (lavender → cyan → acid → coral → dark), ghost clock digits, Geist headlines, the app windows whole, large, sharp.
- **Sound:** calm 100–110 bpm pulse; typing ticks in the hook, a soft pop per landing, music lifts when the canvas zooms out, one bass hit on the tiles.

## A — "One day, one take"

- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day · one continuous take.
- **What you see:** the website's **Today strip** is the world. The red now-line glides from 07:00 to 18:00; at each block (Plan, Calendar, Focus, Habits, Canvas) the camera dives into the block, which opens into that real window doing its thing, then pulls back out onto the strip. Never a cut — the day just flows.
- **First 2 s:** the Today strip fills the screen, `07:00` pill red, the word "Run" block lit; the camera is already pushing into it.
- **Beats:** strip → dive Plan → out → dive Calendar → out → dive Focus (timer runs) → out → dive Habits → out → Canvas pull-back reveals the whole day → claim → tiles.
- **Look:** the site's acid hero ground with the strip, each dive lands on that hour's colour.
- **Sound:** one continuous bed, a whoosh into each dive (rotated takes), ticks only where text types.

## B — "Nine become one" (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** life before and after · split screen to one.
- **What you see:** nine separate little tools (timer, to-dos, habits, calendar, clock, focus sounds, notes, analytics, tracking) as nine panes, all pinging at once — chaos. They collapse into the website's **3D cube**, which turns face by face; each face opens into the real KRNL0 window for that job. Calm after noise.
- **First 2 s:** nine panes already on screen, badges popping, a phone-style notification stack shaking — "Nine apps." in giant type.
- **Beats:** nine panes churning → slam into one point → the cube forms and turns → face: Todos → face: Calendar → face: Timer → face: Habits → the cube settles into the logo pose → "One app instead of nine." → tiles + CTA.
- **Look:** dark noise in the before, the acid ground in the after; the cube is the website's own.
- **Sound:** near-silence with overlapping pings in the before, a hard cut to the bed when the cube forms, riser into the logo.
